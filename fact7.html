Top 7 Cursed Objects That Killed Their Owners

Some objects carry death with them. Touch them and your luck changes forever. People who owned these 7 cursed items died mysteriously. Similar dark energy exists at 666 Park Avenue. Never buy these if you see them.

1. The Dybbuk Box - Wine Cabinet of Death

A Jewish wine box sold on eBay. Every owner got nightmares of a old woman with half face. One owner had a stroke after opening it. Another lost all his hair overnight. The box contains a Dybbuk - evil spirit. It attacks people in sleep. Last owner donated it to a museum. Even there, staff refuse to go near it at night.

2. Annabelle Doll - Real Demon Doll

Not the movie prop. Real Annabelle is in a locked glass case. Two nurses owned her first. The doll moved by itself and wrote "Help Me" on paper. A priest blessed it but died in car crash next day. A man mocked the doll and died in bike accident same night. Ed and Lorraine Warren said demon uses the doll to enter homes.

3. The Crying Boy Painting

1980s UK painting of a sad boy. Houses with this painting caught fire. But the painting never burned. Firefighters found it untouched in ashes. Over 50 house fires reported. Owners said boy in painting cries real tears before fire. Artist died by suicide. His last words: "The boy wanted revenge".

4. Hope Diamond - Blue Death Curse

45 carat blue diamond. Every owner died badly. King Louis XVI and Marie Antoinette - beheaded. Evalyn Walsh McLean - son died age 9, daughter suicide. Harry Winston who donated it to museum - died of heart attack. Diamond glows blood red under UV light. Scientists say it's radiation. Believers say it's cursed blood.

5. Busby Stoop Chair

300 year old chair in England pub. Thomas Busby was hanged in 1702 for murder. His last curse: "Death to anyone who sits in my chair". 63 people who sat in it died within days. Car crashes, heart attacks, suicide. Pub finally hung the chair on wall so no one can sit. Since then, zero deaths.

6. The Basano Vase

15th century Italian silver vase. Made for a bride who died on wedding night holding it. Note inside said "Beware... this vase brings death". 7 families who owned it - all members died within 3 months. First owner - suicide. Second - mysterious illness. Last family buried it in 1988. Police dug it up but refused to touch it.

7. Robert The Doll

100 year old sailor doll in Key West museum. Boy named Robert got it as gift. Soon parents heard their son talking to doll at night. Doll's voice replied. Neighbors saw doll moving window to window when family was out. People who mock Robert or take photo without permission face accidents. Museum gets apology letters every week.

Warning: Cursed objects are real. They hold negative energy like 666 Park Avenue building. If you find any of these, don't touch. Don't buy. Don't even look at photos too long. Curse can travel through images.
