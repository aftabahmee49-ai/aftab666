Unsolved Mysteries of 666 Park Avenue - Full Dark History

1884-1900: The Drake Family Curse

Apple Maps shows wrong address. 2018: NYPD report says "666 Park Avenue does not exist" but building stands. Mail still delivered there. Tax records show $0 value.

Building has 6 floors, 66 windows, built in '66 minutes per floor' - old blueprint says. Math doesn't add up but deaths do.
