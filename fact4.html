Why is 666 Called The Devil's Number? Real History Explained

The number 666 is known worldwide as the "Number of the Beast" from the Book of Revelation. But why is it considered evil?

1. Biblical Origin

In Revelation 13:18, it says "Let the one with understanding calculate the number of the beast, for it is 666." This referred to Emperor Nero using Hebrew numerology.

2. Why Buildings Skip 666

Many buildings skip 666 due to superstition. You won't find 666 Park Avenue in real New York. After 665 comes 668.

3. 666 in Pop Culture

The TV show "666 Park Avenue" used this fear. Movies like "The Omen" made 666 famous as a horror symbol.
