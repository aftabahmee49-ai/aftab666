About Our Horror Research Team

Who Are We?

Welcome to 666 Park Avenue Files. We are a team of independent paranormal researchers based in New York City. Since 2018, we investigate haunted locations, document real ghost stories, and expose dark history hidden from public.

Our Mission

Our goal is simple: Tell the truth about paranormal activity. Every story on this site is researched using police reports, newspaper archives, witness interviews, and historical records. We don't create fake horror. We uncover real horror that mainstream media hides.

Why 666 Park Avenue?

666 Park Avenue is the most haunted address in NYC. 25+ deaths, government cover-up, satanic history. This building connects all NYC ghost stories. Our team spent 6 years researching this location. What we found will shock you.

Our Research Process

1. Field Investigation: We visit locations at night with EMF meters, thermal cameras, audio recorders.
2. Archive Search: We check 100+ year old newspapers, death certificates, police files.
3. Witness Interview: We talk to survivors, neighbors, ex-tenants. No names published without permission.
4. Fact Check: Every claim verified by 2+ sources before publishing.
